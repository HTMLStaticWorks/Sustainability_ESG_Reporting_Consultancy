Procurement Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions for Enterprise Procurement

Technical criteria, engagement timelines, and working paper ownership for corporate legal and finance teams.

We produce standard statutory workpapers formatted under ISAE 3000 (Revised) and ISO 14064-3. During the statutory assurance window, our lead verifiers attend technical audit defense sessions directly with your external financial auditors (PwC, EY, Deloitte, KPMG), responding to sample queries with primary telemetry data.
A full CSRD double-materiality audit and Scope 1–3 telemetry compilation typically requires 10 to 12 weeks from kickoff to Big-4 defense. Emergency fast-track diagnostics for imminent reporting deadlines can be compressed to 6 weeks under accelerated primary data ingestion protocols.
We deploy an automated hybrid approach: spend-based EEIO benchmarks for initial tier mapping, combined with our secure supplier portal for high-impact suppliers. This allows us to achieve over 85% primary and verified secondary coverage, well exceeding statutory materiality thresholds.
Every enterprise engagement is protected by bilateral mutual NDAs. Client data is hosted within ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II compliant tenant vaults in Zurich and Frankfurt with end-to-end encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). No client telemetry is shared or used in public models.
