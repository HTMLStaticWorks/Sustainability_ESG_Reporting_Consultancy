Mandatory EU Directive

Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)

The CSRD requires over 50,000 companies operating in Europe to produce verifiable disclosures across 12 European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS). It mandates formal double materiality — evaluating both environmental impact on society and climate risks to corporate value.

Verdant Assurance Deliverables:
  • Double Materiality Matrix & Stakeholder Survey
  • ESRS E1–E5 Environmental Topic Mapping
  • Limited to Reasonable Assurance Audit Certification
Global Benchmark

Global Reporting Initiative (GRI)

GRI represents the world's most widely adopted public sustainability reporting framework. It emphasizes multi-stakeholder impact accountability, detailing how organizational operations influence economic, environmental, and human rights factors globally.

Verdant Assurance Deliverables:
  • GRI Content Index & Disclosures Alignment
  • Human Rights Due Diligence (GRI 2)
  • Sector-Specific Standards Verification
Carbon Accounting Engine

Greenhouse Gas Protocol (Scope 1, 2, 3)

The GHG Protocol establishes comprehensive global standardized frameworks to measure and manage greenhouse gas emissions from private and public sector operations, value chains, and mitigation actions.

Verdant Assurance Deliverables:
  • Scope 1 Direct Combustion & Fleet Inventory
  • Scope 2 Market-Based vs Location-Based Power Verification
  • Scope 3 Upstream & Downstream Value Chain Modeling
Capital Markets Framework

ISSB S1 & S2 (Incorporating TCFD)

Governed by the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB), these standards establish a comprehensive global baseline of sustainability-related financial disclosures for investors and capital markets.

Verdant Assurance Deliverables:
  • 2°C & 1.5°C Climate Scenario Analysis
  • Physical vs Transition Financial Risk Quantification
  • Executive Board Governance & Oversight Disclosures
