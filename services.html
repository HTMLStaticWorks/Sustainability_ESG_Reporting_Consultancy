EFRAG & ESRS 1 Statutory Core

Materiality Assessment & Double Materiality Matrix

Materiality is the statutory cornerstone of EU CSRD and ESRS reporting. Verdant ESG conducts rigorous Double Materiality Assessments that simultaneously quantify both outside-in Impact Materiality (your enterprise's positive and negative impacts on ecosystems and society) and inside-out Financial Materiality (sustainability risks and opportunities that affect enterprise value, operating margins, and balance sheet resilience).

Double Materiality Dual-Axis Matrix (ESRS 1 Section 3) Threshold Scoring: Scale 1 (Low) to 5 (Critical) across Impact & Financial Axes
Auditor-Validated
Quadrant I: Double Material

Mandatory Statutory Disclosure

Topics exceeding both impact and financial materiality thresholds. Requires full quantitative ESRS disclosure and third-party audit verification.

Scope 1–3 GHG (E1) Water Scarcity (E3) Workforce Safety (S1)
Quadrant II: Financial Material

Enterprise Risk & Transition Hedge

Climate factors threatening cash flows, asset valuation, or debt covenants. Disclosed under ESRS 2 and IFRS S2 financial risk frameworks.

Quadrant III: Impact Material

Societal & Ecosystem Impact

Severe external externalities across upstream suppliers or downstream product end-of-life that demand mitigation disclosures.

Deforestation (EUDR) Packaging Circularity (E5) Community Rights (S3)
Quadrant IV: Monitored

Threshold Logging & Screening

Topics scored below statutory significance cutoff. Logged in audit trail with documented justification papers for Big-4 sign-off.

Office Paper Waste Local Noise Pollution Internal Travel

ESG Data Collection & Scope 1–3 Telemetry Pipeline

Manual spreadsheets are the single largest source of ESG audit failures. Verdant ESG implements an end-to-end, automated data collection pipeline that consolidates raw activity feeds into audit-grade emissions registers. From direct enterprise resource planning (ERP) ledger connectors to automated utility IoT smart meters and secure supplier intake portals, our architecture delivers mathematically verifiable data lineage from ledger entry to final statutory disclosure.

Tier 1: ERP & GL Connectors

Direct API integration with SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite, Coupa, and Workday for automated spend and activity data ingestion.

Automated Financial Connectors

Tier 2: IoT Utility Telemetry

Continuous feeds from electrical smart meters, SCADA substation controllers, and gas flow sensors for real-time Scope 1 & 2 accounting.

Real-Time Sensor Telemetry

Tier 3: Supplier Primary Portal

Air-gapped intake portal for Tier-1 to Tier-4 supply chain vendors, automatically resolving emission factors against DEFRA, Ecoinvent, and Exiobase.
