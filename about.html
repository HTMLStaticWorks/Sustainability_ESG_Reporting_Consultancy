Our Purpose & Heritage

Bridging Corporate Finance with Environmental Science

Founded by former statutory auditors and climate scientists, Verdant ESG exists to bring true scientific integrity and mathematical rigor to corporate sustainability reporting.

The Origin of Audit-Grade ESG

For over a decade, corporate sustainability lived in glossy annual PDF reports with vague commitments and unverified claims. With the passage of the European Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) and global ISSB mandates, the era of self-declared green claims has definitively ended.

Verdant ESG was established to provide the missing layer: third-party certified verification, automated greenhouse gas telemetry, and audit workpapers that satisfy the Big-4 accounting firms and statutory regulators worldwide.
